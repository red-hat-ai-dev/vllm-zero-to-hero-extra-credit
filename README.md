# vLLM Zero to Hero: Extra credit

### *Optional lessons in the vLLM Zero to Hero series. Follow the [campaign path](https://github.com/red-hat-ai-dev/vLLM-zero-to-hero-overview) to learn more.*

Okay, so you've served a model, made it more efficient, and measured how it
performs. What's next from here?

Extra credit is where we explore more of what we can do with vLLM. Each topic
has its own directory, README, and supporting material, so you can pick the
lesson that interests you.

## Choose a topic

| Topic | What you will learn | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **llm-d** | How inference-aware routing, cached prefixes, and separate prefill and decode workers help scale inference. | [**Scale inference with llm-d**](llm-d/README.md) |

The llm-d lesson is a visual explainer. You do not need a Kubernetes cluster or
multiple accelerators to follow along.
